Private Correspondence Vienna · 16 May 2003
To: Herr M. Weiss
Private Collections Office
Vienna

Dear Herr Weiss,

Following our review of the objects retained in the private collection, I am writing regarding the unidentified jewel photographed during the earlier inventory.

The object remains in the Vienna collection. Its previous ownership could not be established, although the accompanying documentation appears to refer to an earlier European private holding.

INVENTORY REFERENCE · V-03-17

Please retain this reference in any future correspondence concerning the object. No revised attribution should be entered until the provenance can be established with greater certainty.

I have enclosed a copy of the earlier collection photograph for the file.

Yours sincerely,

E. Hartmann
Private Collections Office
