COLLECTION & PROVENANCE RECORDS

The Vienna Correspondence Archive preserves selected letters, memoranda and private communications relating to collectors, estates and privately held objects in Vienna and surrounding regions.

The surviving correspondence has been arranged chronologically where dates could be established. Some records remain incomplete.
