INVENTORY REFERENCE · V-03-17

Unidentified Crystal Jewel

Jewellery · Private Collection

Year 2003
Location Vienna
Inventory V-03-17

Description

An unidentified jewel consisting of a central crystal surrounded by five diamonds. The object was retained within a private Vienna collection and photographed during a collection review.

Provenance

Previous ownership could not be established from the surviving documentation. Earlier European private collection records are referenced but not confirmed.

Collection Record

The object remained within the Vienna collection following the 2003 review. No sale or transfer was recorded at the time of documentation.

Status

RETAINED · PROVENANCE INCOMPLETE

Archival Instruction

Over the years, the Vienna collection retained the unusual jewel despite incomplete provenance.
